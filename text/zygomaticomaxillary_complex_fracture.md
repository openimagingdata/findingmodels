# Zygomaticomaxillary complex fracture—`OIFM_OIDM_159654`

**Synonyms:** ZMC fracture, tripod fracture, trimalar fracture

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Fracture pattern involving the zygomaticomaxillary complex, typically affecting the zygomatic arch, orbital rim or wall, and maxillary buttress attachments.
