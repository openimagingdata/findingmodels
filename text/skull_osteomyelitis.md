# Skull osteomyelitis—`OIFM_OIDM_154564`

**Synonyms:** calvarial osteomyelitis, cranial osteomyelitis

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Infection of the skull or calvarial bone, potentially associated with bone erosion, sclerosis, soft-tissue inflammation, or adjacent sinus or scalp infection.
