# Acute subdural hemorrhage—`OIFM_OIDM_368223`

**Synonyms:** acute SDH, acute subdural hematoma

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Acute blood products in the subdural space, typically hyperattenuating on noncontrast CT and often crescentic along the cerebral convexity, falx, or tentorium.
