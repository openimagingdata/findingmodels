# Subarachnoid hemorrhage—`OIFM_OIDM_975386`

**Synonyms:** SAH, subarachnoid blood

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Acute or chronic blood products within the subarachnoid space, including sulci, cisterns, or fissures, commonly visible as hyperattenuation on noncontrast CT in the acute setting.
