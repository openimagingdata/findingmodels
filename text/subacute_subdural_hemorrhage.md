# Subacute subdural hemorrhage—`OIFM_OIDM_362894`

**Synonyms:** subacute SDH, subacute subdural hematoma

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Subacute blood products in the subdural space, typically evolving toward isoattenuation relative to brain parenchyma on CT depending on age of hemorrhage.
