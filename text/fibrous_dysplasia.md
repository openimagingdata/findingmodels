# Fibrous dysplasia—`OIFM_OIDM_341267`

**Synonyms:** fibrous dysplasia skull

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Benign fibro-osseous disorder in which normal bone is replaced by fibrous tissue and immature woven bone, often producing expansile ground-glass osseous change in the skull or facial bones.
