# Cribriform plate fracture—`OIFM_OIDM_587234`

**Synonyms:** cribriform fracture

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Fracture involving the cribriform plate of the ethmoid bone, which may be associated with anterior skull base trauma or cerebrospinal fluid leak.
