# Sutural diastasis—`OIFM_OIDM_213066`

**Synonyms:** diastatic suture, widened suture, suture widening, diastatic fracture, diastatic skull fracture

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Abnormal widening or separation of a cranial suture, often traumatic when associated with skull fracture.
