# Arachnoid granulation—`OIFM_OIDM_836917`

**Synonyms:** Pacchionian granulation

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Benign protrusion of arachnoid membrane into a dural venous sinus or calvarial venous structure, often appearing as a rounded filling defect or CSF-density focus.
