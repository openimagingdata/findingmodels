# Intracranial lipoma—`OIFM_OIDM_078516`

**Synonyms:** meningeal lipoma, lipomatous hamartoma

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

A congenital fat-containing intracranial lesion, usually extra-axial and often located along midline cisternal or pericallosal regions.
