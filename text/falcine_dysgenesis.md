# Falcine dysgenesis—`OIFM_OIDM_211197`

**Synonyms:** absent falx, partial falx, absent falx cerebri, falcine hypoplasia, absent interhemispheric fissure, partial interhemispheric fissure

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Congenital abnormal development or absence of the falx cerebri, often associated with abnormal formation of the interhemispheric fissure.
