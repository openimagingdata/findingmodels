# Brachytherapy seed—`OIFM_OIDM_090347`

**Synonyms:** brachytherapy seeds, radioactive seed, radioactive seeds, I-125 seed, Cs-131 seed, GammaTile, permanent brachytherapy implant

**Tags:** head, CT, finding
