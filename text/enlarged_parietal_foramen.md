# Enlarged parietal foramen—`OIFM_OIDM_956084`

**Synonyms:** biparietal foramina

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Congenital enlargement of one or both parietal foramina near the sagittal suture, often appearing as paired calvarial defects.
