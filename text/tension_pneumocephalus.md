# Tension pneumocephalus—`OIFM_OIDM_553278`

**Synonyms:** tension pneumocranium

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Pneumocephalus under pressure causing mass effect on intracranial structures, classically producing separated frontal lobes or other signs of clinically significant intracranial air tension.
