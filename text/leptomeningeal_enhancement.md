# Leptomeningeal enhancement—`OIFM_OIDM_121473`

**Synonyms:** pial enhancement, pia-arachnoid enhancement

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Abnormal contrast enhancement along the pia and arachnoid surfaces or within the subarachnoid spaces, suggesting inflammatory, infectious, neoplastic, or vascular meningeal involvement.
