# Perihardware lucency—`OIFM_OIDM_328976`

**Synonyms:** lucency around hardware, peri-implant lucency, periprosthetic lucency, peri-hardware lucency

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Lucency surrounding implanted hardware, screw, plate, or prosthetic component, which may suggest loosening, infection, or chronic motion depending on context.
