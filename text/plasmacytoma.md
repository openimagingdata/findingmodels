# Plasmacytoma—`OIFM_OIDM_477248`

**Synonyms:** solitary bone plasmacytoma

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Solitary plasma cell neoplasm involving bone or soft tissue, which may appear as a focal lytic skull lesion when osseous.
