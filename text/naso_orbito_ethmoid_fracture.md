# Naso-orbito-ethmoid fracture—`OIFM_OIDM_919194`

**Synonyms:** NOE fracture

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Fracture complex involving the nasal bones, medial orbital walls, and ethmoid region, often affecting the central midface and medial canthal attachments.
