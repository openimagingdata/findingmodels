# Epidural hemorrhage—`OIFM_OIDM_713125`

**Synonyms:** epidural hematoma, EDH

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Blood products in the epidural space between the inner table of the skull and dura, classically lentiform or biconvex on CT and often related to skull fracture or arterial injury.
