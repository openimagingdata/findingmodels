# Calvarial osteopenia—`OIFM_OIDM_472343`

**Synonyms:** skull osteopenia, calvarial demineralization, thinned calvarium

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

Reduced mineralization or thinning of the calvarium, producing decreased skull bone density or apparent demineralization.
