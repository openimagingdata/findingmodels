# Subdural collection—`OIFM_OIDM_441121`

**Synonyms:** subdural fluid collection, subdural fluid, subdural fluid accumulation

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

A collection located in the subdural space between the dura and arachnoid membranes, which may contain cerebrospinal fluid, blood products, pus, or mixed material depending on context.
