# Intracranial dermoid cyst—`OIFM_OIDM_576584`

**Synonyms:** intracranial dermoid, dermoid tumor

**Tags:** head, CT, finding

A congenital extra-axial inclusion cyst containing dermal elements and lipid-rich material, often located near the midline and potentially associated with fat droplets if ruptured.
